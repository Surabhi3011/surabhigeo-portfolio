Emergency Response Facility Optimization

Tech
SQL, spatial analysis
Type
Personal project

Problem

Placing emergency response facilities well means balancing where people live against where incidents actually happen — a decision that's hard to make well from intuition alone.

Approach

Used SQL-based spatial analysis to evaluate candidate facility locations against population density and historical incident data.

  • Joined population density data with historical incident records spatially.
  • Scored candidate facility locations by proximity-weighted coverage of demand.
  • Surfaced the placement options that minimized average response distance.

Outcome

A data-driven method for comparing facility placement options, replacing guesswork with a repeatable SQL-based scoring approach grounded in real population and incident data.
