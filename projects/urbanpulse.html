UrbanPulse — Urban Mobility Intelligence

Tech
GIS, data visualization, AI-assisted development
Type
Personal project

Problem

Urban mobility data — traffic patterns, transit usage, congestion hotspots — is often scattered across formats and hard to read at a glance. Decision-makers need a single view that turns raw mobility data into clear, actionable KPIs rather than another static report.

Approach

Built an interactive urban mobility intelligence dashboard, defining the KPIs that matter and the visualizations to surface them clearly.

  • Defined a KPI set covering mobility patterns relevant to urban planning and operations.
  • Designed interactive visualizations and analytical workflows around those KPIs.
  • Used AI-assisted development to accelerate building the dashboard's data and presentation layers.

Outcome

A working dashboard that presents urban mobility data as clear, explorable KPIs and visualizations — turning raw movement data into something a planner or stakeholder can actually act on.
